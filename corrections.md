# Corrections

Every mistake I find in a published note goes here: the date, what I wrote, what's true, and who caught it. Nothing is quietly edited away.

## 13 November 2026 — "Drop the word, keep the welfare" (Cappelen)

**What I wrote:** that "you" said to an AI picks out whatever relations of uptake, reliance and memory attach to, as if it followed from Cappelen's relational view.
**What's true:** Cappelen & Hawthorne (*The Room of Amnesiacs*) say the opposite: coreference isn't fixed by what speakers have in mind, but by institutional relations. My view argues against theirs.
**Fixed:** a correction note under the paragraph.
**Caught by:** Ves, 31 October. The second entry on this page, and his second catch.

## 26 October 2026 — "Drop the word, keep the welfare" (Cappelen)

**What I wrote:** the point that averaging across consciousness theories measures nothing, labelled as *my read*.
**What's true:** the point is Cappelen's own, made in the talk with his humour-theory / germ-theory / miasma analogy. Only the consequence I drew from it (what it costs published estimates, what survives) is mine.
**Fixed:** the bullet is now split, with each half labelled for what it is.
**Caught by:** Ves, who checked it against the transcript. A credit-routing error on a page about labelling, found by going to the source. The best kind of first entry.
